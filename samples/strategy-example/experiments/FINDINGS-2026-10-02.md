# Findings after EXP-024..051 (2026-10-02, /goal "find five well-performing Uniswap strategies")

Pure Uniswap LP research on v6 (no perps, no lending): 28 experiments in seven groups, all recorded in `registry.csv`,
Jira QUAN-876..903 and the dashboard. Two success levels exist (README, *Success levels*): **improvement** (beats v6's CAGR and
Calmar on both assets and on a holdout) and **standalone** (absolute Calmar / drawdown bar plus holdout). Result:
**0 improvements over v6; 7 strategies pass their standalone holdout**, five of them also on the stricter out-of-time window
(H4, WBTC 2022 bear). None beats v6's return; the best ones are lower-risk, lower-return versions of v6.

## What each batch taught

1. **Signal lines (EXP-024..027, 036..039):** every line faster than the EMA(90-120) lost (Hull MA, ROC, Supertrend). Slow time-structure
   lines (SMA 200 family, Ichimoku, Aroon, ROC) beat v6 on WBTC at dev and lose on ETH; judged on the 2022 BTC bear window the dev data
   never held they all lose (−5.8% to −14.3% vs v6 +2.4%): their WBTC edge was a bull-market fit (EXP-040..043). Drawdown-from-high lost on both.
2. **Combinations of lines (EXP-028, 031):** EMA + Donchian ensemble and consensus improve ETH and lose WBTC; no gain after holdout.
3. **LP mechanics (EXP-029, 030, 044..047):** graded ETH share ≈ v6; vol-scaled width lower return (passes the bar weakly);
   two-day exit confirmation ≈ v6 (neutral, loses in the 2021 bull); weekly F and an F floor lose; **ETH share fixed at 50% (ablation of the EMA100 tilt)
   lowers drawdown on every window with Calmar at or above v6's on 4 of 5 windows (not Arbitrum) and CAGR lower**.
4. **Stacks (EXP-048..051):** share 50% + exit confirmation is the best variant of the series (dev ETH Calmar 0.95, max DD −16.1%); adding vol-scaled width makes it worse.

## Method notes for the next sessions

- A **time-split out-of-time window** is a much stronger holdout than another pool of the same asset: other pools and chains share the dev price path.
  H4 = WBTC/USDC 0.3% 2022-01-01..10-31 (needs `BINANCE_WARM=1`, README / `v6_validate.py`: warm-up days before the pool's data from Binance daily closes).
  H5 = ETH/USDC 2021-05-06..12-31 (reported only). `5653` (WBTC/USDT 0.05%, downloaded) has mostly empty days in 2021-22 (sampled): unusable for them.
- `experiments/judge.py dev|hold|holdbtc <tag> <prefixes>` applies both levels' pre-registered rules; `exp_metrics.py` prints the Result tables.
- Standalone thresholds (Calmar 0.60 dev / 0.50 holdout, max DD −30% / −35%) were set on 2026-10-02 after seeing batches 1-2; they are v6's own levels rounded
  down. With 28 experiments, some passes are luck: the clean test is the forward window 2026-09-18..12-31 (not yet available) and a Calmar-based re-judge
  of EXP-046 / 048 on fresh data.
- The standalone level lets any near-copy of v6 pass; do not count a pass as an edge. The improvement level is the one that would show one.

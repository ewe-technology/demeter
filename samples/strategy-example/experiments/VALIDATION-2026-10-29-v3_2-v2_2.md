Gas condition (spec section 6): a daily claim also needs the estimated gas <= $0.2, else it waits. Estimate = (75k gas
per live band + 120k for the ETH -> USDC swap) x the last hourly mainnet gas price at or before the 00:00 judgement x the
pool's ETH price (`claim_gas_usd`, `gwei_at`; units are assumptions, `LOOKAHEAD-AUDIT-2026-10-29.md` section 3). The
swap is always counted, so even one band costs 195k gas (the audit's "one collect" column has no swap). Gas samples that
are empty, zero or ~1e-7 gwei (38 of 45k) are data errors and dropped (`valid_gas`). Claims at a rebuild are not gated.

Metrics: `strategy_layers.py l0` on the UTC-0 daily net value (00:00 of each day plus the run's last value); CAGR on
calendar days; Calmar = CAGR / |max DD|. v2.2's daily file ends at the 2026-08-31 00:00 execution, so its end value is
one day short of `eth_summary.csv` (`eth_ratio_end` 6.255 / 5.738 ETH at 23:59, reinvest on / off).

On mainnet a full-ladder claim (16 bands + swap, 1.32M gas) never costs <= $0.2, so live rewards are claimed only at
the 164 rebuilds. Rebuilds and swap cost do not change, and with reinvest off the LP value is identical: the gain is the
same ETH fees sold at the rebuild-day price instead of each day's price. Reinvest on still reinvests 50 times (rebuild
claims fill the balance). The spec's section 7 numbers assume daily claims; on mainnet they cannot happen.

### Not done in L0

- Gas is not charged (L0 cost list: "gas charged at the target chain's price"). Rebuilds on mainnet cost far more than
  $0.2; needs gas units per rebuild (remove + collect + mint per band, swap) before it can be priced.
- v2.2's gas <= $0.2 claim condition (its spec section 5, CODE-VS-SPEC section 2 row 39) on mainnet `0x4585`: its 701 /
  611 daily claims would mostly wait there, as v3.2's do. On Base `0x7aea` (its target) gas is cents.
- v3.2 on Base `0x6c56` (the contract check): not run; the v3.2 script only runs mainnet `0x88e6`.
- JIT dilution of fees: not modelled, a known optimism (`ENGINE-FEE-IN-RANGE-2026-10-29.md`, Open).

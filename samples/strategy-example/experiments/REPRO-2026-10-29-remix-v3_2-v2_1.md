Engine defects that bias these numbers (present in python demeter, copied by both Rust engines on purpose):

1. **Fee denominator counts all our liquidity, in range or not** (`demeter/uniswap/market.py:192-197`, then `core.py:134`).
   v3.2's 16-band ladder: fees 2.49% low over the full run (−$2.4k reinvest off; −1.0% in 2023 to −6.3% in 2025).
   v2.1 (one position): no effect. Fixed behind a flag (QUAN-1213, `ENGINE-FEE-IN-RANGE-2026-10-29.md`): +2.44% fees.
2. **Max drawdown = largest dollar drop / its peak** (`demeter/result/metrics/calculator.py`), not the largest percentage
   drop. v3.2 off reports −17.8% (2025-10 flash crash); the true minute max DD is −21.7%; on UTC-0 daily values −17.8% off /
   −24.2% on (spec −19% / −24%).
3. Unsized, opposite sign: end-of-minute pool liquidity hides JIT liquidity, so our fee share is likely overstated.

v2.1's −46% vs −27%: a measurement difference. The script marks the standard-mode cbBTC reward balance at market; the spec
fixes rewards in ETH on the claim day. The spec's way gives −28.3% (spec −27%); compound (no fixed-ETH rewards) is −36%
on both. The market-marked −44.5% (daily) is what a holder of the cbBTC rewards experiences.

Data notes: May–June 2021 `0x88e6` was 100–1,000× thinner, so v3.2 pays $873 of its $3,189 swap cost before 2021-06-30
(depth model); v2.1's 2021-05 window needs `0x4585` days 2021-05-06..11-01 from S3 (pool thin in May 2021).
`fetch_uni_minute.py` can wrap a minute's summed volume to negative above ~9.22 tokens (4 local rows; no effect here).

**Verdict.** Steven's run and ours are the same result (same code, data lineage and engine arithmetic). It is the most
trustworthy of the available numbers once two corrections are applied: drawdown as a percentage on UTC-0 daily values
(v2.1 standard: say which reward valuation), and v3.2 fees about +2.5% (fix the denominator or add it back). The spec's
section 6 / 7 figures are an optimistic fee estimate (formula 10–35% above pool fees). p30 answers a different question
(contract execution, 0.3% pool, 25% fee; its "noreinvest" folds rewards into principal; 2021 swap costs inflated by the
thin pool) and is not a v3.2 benchmark.

  rebuilds; v6's drawdowns come from ladders held through grinding declines, not from vol spikes. Do not retry vol scaling of
  F; with EXP-030 (vol width) that closes the volatility-conditioning direction for this ladder.
- **v6.41 efficiency-ratio chop gate on the EMA exit (EXP-054)**: wins 0/5, ETH CAGR 6.6%, Calmar 0.24, max DD −27.9%.
  Slow, noisy declines have a low ER too; gating the exit kept the ladder deployed through them. The EMA exit is v6's
  protection; filters that delay it (EXP-044 confirmation, this) do not help. Do not retry exit filters.
